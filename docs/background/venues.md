## Recommendation (submission order)
1. **Climate Services**: best scope match (forecast value for users, seasonal forecast plus sowing decision, benchmark against the
   official forecast). Gold OA APC ~USD 1,500 (nv) is small against the 200 M VND funding if registered before publication.
2. **Journal of Hydrology**: safest Q1 for the forecast-skill/conformal core; hybrid, so no fee. Higher desk-reject risk;
   sharpen the novelty claim (calibrated exceedance probabilities + coverage under leave-station-out).
3. **Environmental Modelling & Software** (or **Climate Risk Management** if the paper ends up decision-heavy). EM&S if the benchmark protocol,
   code and atlas are the stronger story; CRM if the cost-loss and sowing sections dominate.
Avoid HESS/NHESS (public discussion = preprint, 80%) and HSJ (Q2 by publisher page).
