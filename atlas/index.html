About & data

  • Target: maximum salinity of the next 10-day period (dekad) at each station, issued with each NCHMF bulletin.
  • Inputs available at issue time only: lagged discharge, last dekad's maximum, astronomical tide.
  • Model: histogram gradient boosting with quantiles; conformalised quantile regression (CQR) gives 90% intervals; exceedance probabilities for 1, 2 and 4 g/L.
  • Tests: forward-by-season (shown), leave-station-out and leave-province-out coverage; verdicts follow criteria fixed before scoring (protocol v1).
  • Benchmarks: persistence, station climatology, the official NCHMF forecast; uncertainty from paired bootstrap over issue dates (2,000 resamples, 95%).
